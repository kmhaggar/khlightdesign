Hallway Light Installation

Animated Entrance Hall Light

An animated light installation for a residential hallway, designed to create a dynamic and welcoming experience for visitors. The piece features a series of LED lamps that respond to movement, creating a captivating visual display.

Table Light

A distinct and unusual table with eclectic shades

A distinct and unusual table with eclectic shades, designed to create a warm cosy light. A subtle LED accent runs around the edge plinth creating some colour and interest.

Modern Statement Light

Fluorescent Chandelier

A dramatic centre piece casting a diffused light across a large open space. The chandelier is constructed from a series of fluorescent tubes, each individually addressable to create static, colour accented or dynamic lighting effects.

LED Wall Light

Playful, Colourful Wall light

A playful wall light installation designed to create a dynamic and fun experience. The piece features a series of LED lights that change colour and pattern in response to movement, creating a captivating visual display.

Outdoor Light Sculpture

Garden Ring Sculpture

A freestanding timber and steel light sculpture with addressable LEDs and softly glowing suspended elements. The piece was designed as a framed view through the garden by day and an animated focal point after dark.
