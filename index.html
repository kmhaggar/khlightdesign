About

Light, structure and subtle interaction.

Kevin Haggar Light Design creates bespoke illuminated installations that combine engineering, craftsmanship and atmospheric lighting design.

From contemporary garden features to winter trail concepts, each piece is developed to complement its setting while giving visitors a clear focal point and a sense of discovery.

Design approach

Designed for atmosphere, adaptability and public settings.

  • Freestanding concepts for temporary outdoor installation.
  • Low-voltage systems suitable for public environments.
  • Subtle ambient modes through to playful visitor engagement.
  • Scalable forms adaptable to individual routes and landscapes.
  • Designed to complement architectural and garden settings rather than dominate them.
